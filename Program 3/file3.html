ICET

Ilahia College of Engineering and Technology (ICET) is a well-established self-financing co-educational minority institution. It was founded in 2002 by the Ilahia Trust with a sprawling 32-acre campus located in Mulavoor, Muvattupuzha, within the Ernakulam district of Kerala. The institution is approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), New Delhi, and is affiliated with APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University (KTU).
